# Eyelash Extension Training Curriculum
*Note: This course provides the foundational knowledge and safety protocols required for lash artistry. True mastery requires 50 to 100 hours of active, hands-on practice to develop the necessary fine motor skills and muscle memory.*

---

## Part 1: Core Theory and Setup

### Module 1: Lash Fundamentals & Anatomy
Eyelash extensions are single synthetic fibers glued to a person's real eyelashes to make them look longer and thicker. The golden rule is safety: the glue and the extension must never touch the skin.

*   **The Spacing Rule:** Always leave a tiny 1 to 2 millimeter gap between the eyelid and the glue to prevent irritation and allow natural movement.
*   **The Growth Cycle:** Humans lose 1 to 4 lashes every day naturally. 
    *   You can only apply extensions to "teenager" lashes (lashes that have stopped growing and are resting for 2 to 3 weeks). 
    *   You cannot put extensions on "baby" growing lashes because they are too weak and the weight will cause damage.

### Module 2: Tools, Setup, and Hygiene
You are working very close to a person's eyes, so you must use the right tools and keep everything perfectly clean to stop eye infections.

*   **The Toolkit:** You need two pairs of tweezers (one to separate the real lashes, one to place the fake lash), lash primer, strong professional glue, and professional glue remover.
*   **Cleaning Levels:** 
    *   *Sanitize:* Wipe your bed and station with alcohol.
    *   *Disinfect:* Soak your tools in hospital-grade liquid to kill most bacteria.
    *   *Sterilize:* Boil metal tools for 20 minutes to completely kill all living germs.

---

## Part 2: Practice and Application

### Module 3: The Practice Bridge
Before touching a real person, you must train your hands to stay steady and build muscle memory.

*   **Tool Control:** Learn how to hold your tweezers properly to avoid hand cramps during long appointments.
*   **Glue Control:** Learn exactly how much glue to use. Too little, and the lash falls off. Too much, and the lashes stick together in a hard, damaging lump.
*   **Sponge and Mannequin Drills:** Practice picking up extensions and placing them straight up on a makeup sponge. Then, practice on a plastic mannequin head to learn how to isolate one single lash at a time.

### Module 4: Live Application and Safe Removal
When you move to a live model, making sure the client is safe and comfortable is your top priority.

*   **Prep and Taping:** Put soft gel pads under the eyes and use medical tape to cover the bottom lashes so you do not accidentally glue their eyes shut.
*   **The Application Steps:** Use your left hand to separate just one real eyelash. Use your right hand to dip an extension into the glue and gently place it on the isolated real lash.
*   **Safe Removal:** Never pull or pick off old extensions. Apply a special professional glue remover, wait exactly 5 minutes, and gently slide the old extensions off to protect the natural lash.

---

## Part 3: Troubleshooting and Business

### Module 5: Fixing Problems and Aftercare
Things will inevitably go wrong when you are learning. You must know how to fix these issues and how to teach your clients to care for their lashes.

*   **Fixing Stickies:** If two real lashes get glued together, you must carefully pull them apart immediately. If left stuck together, it will pull and hurt the client when the natural lashes try to grow at different speeds.
*   **Client Rules:** Clients must gently wash their lashes every night with a special, oil-free lash soap. They cannot use oily makeup or lotions, because oil melts the lash glue and causes premature shedding.

### Module 6: The Lash Business
You need to turn this new technical skill into a profitable and safe business.

*   **Client Forms:** Have every client sign an intake form and waiver stating they understand the rules, risks, and aftercare procedures before you start working.
*   **Finding Clients:** Set up simple, targeted social media ads on Facebook and Instagram to show off pictures of your best work to potential clients in your local area.

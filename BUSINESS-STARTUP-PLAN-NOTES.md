# Solo Lash Business Startup Plan: what was added to the PDF

Source: "LBS ACADEMY LASH BS. PLAN.pdf" (Lashes by Shazz, "Solo Business Startup Plan", 3 pages). The PDF is a
template: 8 section headings with 3 or 4 one-line prompts each. Every prompt is kept word for word in bold at the
start of its part of a lesson. Everything else in the lessons is added explanation. Please skim these points:

## Changed from the PDF wording
- The PDF is written for a general esthetician ("facials, skin treatments, waxing", "skincare products",
  "your esthetician business"). The course is for a lash business, so service examples are lash services
  (full sets, refills, removal, extras) and "esthetician" is dropped. The lesson says so openly.
- "Sole proprietorship, LLC, partnership": LLC is explained as the US name for a limited company.
- "Necessary local, state, and federal registrations" became "local and national registrations" (Kenya has
  counties, not states).

## Kenya-specific points added (please confirm they match how you advise students)
- Business names/companies are registered with the Business Registration Service on eCitizen.
- A KRA PIN, a county business permit, and any county public health licence/inspection for beauty premises.
  The lesson says this is not legal advice and tells students to confirm with their county. No fees are quoted.
- Business money kept separate, e.g. a business M-Pesa till; till statements as revenue records.

## Practical guidance added (general business practice, no facts or figures invented)
- How to choose a name, writing a mission, goals with a number and a date, what a USP can be for a lash artist.
- A simple customer survey and a competitor table; pricing from costs + time + market; offers must still cover costs.
- Branding consistency, four marketing channels explained, a retention list (book the next refill, reminders,
  loyalty card, client records).
- Booking policies (deposit, lateness, cancellation), equipment vs consumables, four workspace options.
- Startup expenses including a "safety cushion"; revenue forecast; break-even; a simple P&L.
- Insurance types explained; hygiene routine to write into the plan (matches the Shazz lash courses).

## Numbers
- The only numbers are in the break-even and P&L practice examples (refill 2,000, materials 300, monthly costs
  17,000, startup 51,000; P&L 40,000 - 28,000; exam: 15,000 / 1,500). They are labelled "made-up figures, for
  practice only" in the lesson. They are not market prices.
